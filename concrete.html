Concrete & Outdoor Projects

Precision concrete work and premium outdoor living spaces — built for Georgia's climate and designed to last decades.

Concrete Specialists

Concrete That Performs as Good as It Looks

Georgia's climate is tough on concrete — the heat, humidity, and freeze-thaw cycles demand expert-level mix design, proper reinforcement, and precision finishing. T Square Remodeling brings all three.

From basic flatwork to stamped concrete driveways, retaining walls, and full outdoor living spaces — every project is engineered to last 30+ years and look great doing it.

Driveways

Standard, exposed aggregate, stamped, and decorative concrete driveways.

Patios

Custom patio pours with decorative finishes, borders, and integrated drains.

Retaining Walls

Concrete block, poured, and segmental retaining walls for erosion control.

Flatwork

Sidewalks, walkways, slabs, and commercial flatwork to spec.

Outdoor Living

Outdoor Spaces That Extend Your Living Area

Georgia's climate is perfect for outdoor living 9 months of the year. Let us build you an outdoor space that competes with any indoor room.

Outdoor Kitchens

Built-in grills, countertops, sinks, refrigerators, and pergola covers — the ultimate outdoor entertaining space.

Pool Decks & Surrounds

Textured, slip-resistant concrete pool decks and decorative surrounds that handle Georgia heat and heavy use.

Hardscaping & Paving

Pavers, natural stone, brick, and decorative hardscaping solutions for driveways, walkways, and outdoor rooms.
