Our Story

Built From the Ground Up — Same as Every Project We Touch

T Square Remodeling Inc. started with one crew and one goal: show up, do the work, and treat every client like their project is the most important thing on the schedule. That part hasn't changed.

Over 20 years, we've grown into one of Georgia's most trusted full-service construction companies — 850+ completed projects across residential, commercial, remodeling, and concrete work.

From custom homes in Warner Robins and Alpharetta to commercial builds in Macon and Columbus — our reputation isn't built on marketing. It's built on people referring their friends to us.
