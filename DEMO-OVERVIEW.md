## SEO Strategy

### Local SEO Structure
The site is built to rank for *service + location* keyword combinations across Georgia. Examples:

- "residential construction Warner Robins GA"
- "commercial general contractor Macon Georgia"
- "kitchen remodel contractor near me Georgia"
- "concrete driveway Columbus GA"
- "construction company Valdosta GA"

### How It Works
1. **Dedicated service pages** — each service ranks independently instead of competing with each other on one page
2. **Service areas page** — 28 cities listed with zip codes and county names, all of which are indexable signals
3. **Blog content** — two full articles already written; new articles published monthly compound rankings over time
4. **Internal linking** — every page links to related pages, distributing SEO value across the site
5. **Schema markup** — Google reads structured data on the homepage that identifies the business type, location, service area, phone number, hours, and aggregate rating

### Schema Markup Included
- `GeneralContractor` — business identity, contact, hours, service area
- `FAQPage` — 5 questions Google can pull directly into search results
- `AggregateRating` — star rating display in search results
- `Article` — on both blog posts for rich result eligibility
- `Service` — on the residential and commercial service pages
